Who we are

MUP Trading is a Giza-based joint stock company, founded in 2025 to give quality medicines a clear, compliant route to Egyptian patients, from the manufacturer’s dock to the pharmacy shelf.

What we do

Trading
Wholesale and retail supply of medicines, medical supplies and healthcare products.
Import and export
Sourcing from qualified international manufacturers and bringing their products to market in Egypt.
Product registration
Preparing dossiers and securing registrations and approvals with the Egyptian authorities.
Contract manufacturing
Producing our formulations with licensed third-party facilities, under our quality oversight.
Marketing and distribution
Promoting and delivering products, our own and our partners’, to pharmacies, hospitals and distributors.
